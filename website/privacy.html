Privacy Notice and Data Consent

Effective October 30, 2026 · Current development version

Current local development version. Guest and registered screening records remain on the phone. Cloud screening storage and Firebase integration are planned, not enabled. Opening this page does not record consent or change your account.

Effective date: October 30, 2026

Responsible operator: Karl Christopher D. Blockstock, Michelle Ann S. Angeles, Emmanuel M. Espeña and Gerald Elli Ramos of the College of Computer Studies, Technological Institute of the Philippines–Quezon City.

Privacy contact: qmasamar@tip.edu.ph

1. Coverage

This notice explains how DermaSight processes information through its mobile application, local guest sessions and registered accounts. Cloud screening storage is not implemented in the current version.

Philippine privacy law requires personal-data processing to follow the principles of transparency, legitimate purpose and proportionality. Health-related information is subject to the requirements governing sensitive personal information.

2. Information we process

Depending on the features you use, DermaSight processes:

Personal information: Name, age, and sex, when provided.

Screening information: Selected lesion location, occupation, sunburn history, symptoms, and checklist responses.

Photographs: Skin lesion photographs captured through the camera or selected from the device gallery, including image copies prepared for classification.

Screening results: Screening identifiers, dates, possible lesion classifications, confidence scores, and displayed recommendations.

Account information: Email address, account identifiers, profile details, and information required for authentication, if online accounts are enabled.

Technical information: Connection and diagnostic information necessary to operate, maintain, and secure online services, where collected.

Camera and photo-library permissions allow you to capture or select images. Only provide information and photographs you are authorized to use.

3. Why we process this information

DermaSight processes information for the following purposes:

To prepare lesion images and generate preliminary classification results.

To record the screening information provided by the user.

To save and retrieve screening history on the device.

To create and authenticate online accounts, where applicable.

Cloud screening storage is planned and unavailable in the current version. Screening records are saved and retrieved locally.

To operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and secure the application and its services.

Information must not be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis and any additional consent required.

4. Offline processing and storage

In offline mode, DermaSight processes lesion photographs on the device using its integrated machine-learning model. Screening records created in offline mode are stored locally, according to the application's implemented storage functions.

Offline screening does not automatically upload records when the device reconnects to the internet. An existing offline record will be transferred to online storage only when a separate transfer or upload feature is available and the user is informed of the transfer before it occurs.

Local records may become unavailable if application data is removed, the device is lost or damaged, or access to the relevant local account or storage is lost. Offline records should not be assumed to have a cloud backup.

5. Online accounts and cloud storage

Screening runs on the device for guests and registered users. Online account functions require an internet connection. Cloud screening storage is a future feature, not an active part of this version.

When users register or sign in, account information is sent to the project account server. It currently runs on the project Mac and stores profiles, linked Google identities, salted password hashes and authentication sessions in SQLite. Screening photos and results are not uploaded, including when the phone is online. Local SQLite ownership identifies a guest session or permanent user ID; email is not used as a substitute for ownership checks.

An email address may be used to identify an online account. Saving a screening does not authorize automatic distribution of screening reports by email.

The following deployment details must reflect the actual released application:

Account service: the project Node.js account backend with SQLite and Google identity-token verification. Firebase Authentication is not configured in this version.

Cloud-storage provider: none configured. Any future provider, database and image-storage services must be disclosed before upload is enabled.

Hosting country or region: the development account server runs on the project Mac; a production hosting country or region has not been finalized. No Firebase storage deployment is configured.

Authorized recipients and administrators: Authorized users, designated project administrators, and service providers may access personal information only as necessary for the disclosed purposes and subject to the applicable access controls and privacy requirements.

Any cross-border processing: Personal information may be processed or stored outside the Philippines depending on the configuration and infrastructure of the third-party services used. The applicable countries or regions and safeguards must be confirmed before deployment.

6. Security

Current development implementation:

The model processes photographs on the phone. Screening records are stored in local SQLite, and images are local files. Online account operations send account information to the account server; there is no online screening upload feature. Session credentials use the platform secure credential store. Development account access currently uses local-network HTTP; production HTTPS is not yet configured. Do not assume end-to-end encryption or encrypted screening databases.

Access to offline records depends on the device's security settings and the application's implemented access controls. Access to online records depends on the authentication mechanisms, account permissions, administrative controls, and service-provider safeguards actually implemented.

Users are advised to protect their devices and online accounts, use strong passwords and available authentication protections, and avoid leaving screening information visible on shared devices.

The project team must verify the protections used for transmitting and storing information before claiming that specific safeguards, such as encryption, are implemented. Local or cloud storage alone does not guarantee encryption or prevent unauthorized access.

Security measures must correspond to the actual released application and its configuration. DermaSight provides preliminary screening information and is not a substitute for professional medical diagnosis.

7. Retention and deletion

Local records: screenings and saved image copies remain on the phone until removed through the available record/history controls or application data removal. There is no automatic expiry or cloud backup. Signing out preserves saved history. Contact the privacy contact for assistance with deletion requests.

Cloud screening records: none are stored by this version. Before any future upload feature is enabled, the provider, retention criteria, separate consent and deletion procedure must be established and disclosed.

Account information: Account information is retained for as long as necessary for the disclosed account-management and application purposes, subject to applicable legal requirements and the documented account-deletion policy. The procedure for deleting an account and its associated information must be explained to users.

Backups and technical logs: Backups, diagnostic logs, and other technical records are retained according to the actual configuration and documented retention policies. Where these contain personal information, the applicable deletion arrangements and any limitations must be disclosed.

Deletion-request procedure: users may submit requests to Michelle Ann S. Angeles at qmasamar@tip.edu.ph. Requests will be reviewed and handled according to the applicable procedures and legal requirements. Where applicable, the project team will identify the affected local and online records, coordinate the appropriate deletion steps, and communicate the outcome and any relevant limitations.

If cloud storage is introduced later, deleting a local copy must not be assumed to delete a cloud copy. Signing out is not a deletion request. Deleting application records does not necessarily delete original gallery photographs, exported reports, screenshots, or copies retained elsewhere. Users must manage these copies separately where applicable.

Retention periods and deletion procedures must be documented and appropriate to the declared purposes and applicable legal requirements. The project team must establish the actual retention periods and deletion arrangements before this notice is finalized.

8. Choices and data-subject rights

Users may decline consent and choose not to proceed with functions that require the relevant processing. Choosing offline screening does not, by itself, authorize uploading screening records to cloud storage.

Subject to applicable law, users may exercise their rights concerning their personal information, including requesting access or correction, objecting to processing, withdrawing consent where consent is the legal basis, and requesting erasure or blocking where applicable.

Privacy requests may be directed to the designated privacy contact identified in this notice. Users may also raise concerns with the Philippine National Privacy Commission.

Withdrawing consent may prevent future use of the affected feature. The project team should explain the effect on existing records and any applicable retention obligations. Withdrawal does not necessarily invalidate processing already lawfully performed.

9. Research, Publication and Future Model Training

The consent requested in this notice covers only the purposes described in this document. It does not automatically authorize the public release of identifiable screening information or the use of submitted photographs and screening records for future model training.

Any additional use for research, publication, or model training must be appropriately disclosed and supported by the applicable legal basis, consent where required, and institutional research or ethics approval where applicable.

10. Information Relating to Minors or Another Person

Eligibility, guardian authorization and assisted-use arrangements:

DermaSight must be used in accordance with the project's approved eligibility, consent, and privacy procedures. Before information relating to minors or another person is collected, the project team must establish and follow the applicable authorization and consent requirements, including parent or legal guardian authorization where required.

Users must not submit another person's photographs or personal information without appropriate authorization. Screening involving minors or another person must follow the institution's approved research, ethics, and privacy procedures.

The project's eligibility rules and guardian-consent arrangements must be confirmed with the capstone adviser before deployment.

11. Consent

Screening consent

“I have read this Privacy Notice. I understand the personal information and photographs DermaSight processes, the purposes of processing, the storage arrangements, and the choices available to me. I voluntarily consent to the processing described in this notice for the screening functions I choose to use.”

Future online cloud-storage consent (not active in this version)

“I understand that when I choose to save an online screening, the relevant screening information, associated lesion photograph, and classification result may be transmitted to the configured cloud-storage service and linked to my account. I have been informed of the applicable storage arrangements and consent to this processing as described in this notice.”

No cloud-upload checkbox is requested in the current app because that feature is unavailable. If cloud storage is introduced, the quoted consent must be reviewed and requested separately when the user chooses upload. Offline screening remains distinct.
