Medical Disclaimer

Preliminary educational screening

Current local development version. Guest and registered screening records remain on the phone. Cloud screening storage and Firebase integration are planned, not enabled. Opening this page does not record consent or change your account.

DermaSight is an academic mobile screening application intended to support awareness and documentation of pigmented skin lesions.

Its outputs are software-generated estimates. The application does not establish a diagnosis, confirm or exclude cancer, assess tumor invasion, determine disease severity or clinical stage, prescribe medication, or provide a treatment plan.

Professional assessment remains necessary

Suspected skin cancer requires assessment by an appropriate healthcare professional. A biopsy and examination of tissue may be necessary to establish the diagnosis. An application prediction cannot replace that process.

Do not postpone consultation, stop prescribed treatment or disregard professional advice because of a DermaSight result.

Possible classifications and confidence scores

A predicted category indicates a possible match among the model’s supported classes. A confidence percentage is not a confirmed diagnosis, a severity percentage or an individually validated probability of cancer.

Any displayed “high,” “medium” or “low” indicator is a software-generated screening label. It does not establish a clinical stage or guarantee that a lesion is harmless.

A low-confidence, inconclusive or apparently reassuring result does not exclude a condition requiring professional assessment.

Body location and personal information

DermaSight permits users to select different lesion locations and record information such as occupation, sunburn history and symptoms.

These entries help document the screening context. They do not prove that every body location or patient group has been validated, or that the model has incorporated each answer into its prediction.

Image and model limitations

Lighting, focus, framing, camera characteristics and image preparation can affect the information available to the model.

The classifier was trained on dermoscopic research images. Performance on ordinary smartphone photographs and across skin tones requires appropriate evaluation. Equal performance across populations should not be assumed.

The supported categories do not cover every skin disease. An unsupported condition can still receive a model prediction. Image-quality or skin-presence checks do not guarantee that an image is suitable for classification.

When to seek assessment

Arrange professional assessment for a new or changing spot, a lesion that looks different from others, persistent itching or pain, bleeding, or a sore that does not heal. Melanoma may occur without pain or discomfort.

ABCDE information is an awareness aid for melanoma. It is not a universal severity scale for every lesion category, and the absence of obvious ABCDE features does not establish that a lesion is harmless.

Offline and online use

The medical limitations apply to guest and registered use. Online account access does not mean a dermatologist has reviewed the photograph or result. Cloud screening storage is not available in the current version.

Emergency situations

DermaSight does not provide emergency assessment, continuously monitored medical support or guaranteed clinician review. If you believe you have a medical emergency, contact appropriate emergency services or attend an emergency facility.
