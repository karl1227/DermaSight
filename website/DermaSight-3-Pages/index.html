About DermaSight

AI-assisted skin lesion awareness

Development version · Updated October 30, 2026

Describes the current prototype. Public contact and hosting details are pending finalization by the project team.

Purpose and workflow

DermaSight supports educational screening through information entry, symptoms, capture/gallery selection, image checks, automatic lesion-region processing, on-device classification and local history. It does not provide a final medical diagnosis.

Guest and registered use

Guests can screen without creating an account. Registered accounts persist on the account server, while screening history remains on the device where it was saved. Creating a new account from guest mode can preserve that session’s scans. Local history is not a cross-device cloud backup.

Phone storage, account server and cloud

Screenings and images stay on the phone. A separate SQLite account database runs on the project Mac in the current development setup. Google Sign-In verifies identity; it is not cloud storage for scans. No cloud backup or automatic cross-device history synchronization is configured.

These three public information pages can be hosted independently of the app and account server. Publishing the pages does not publish account records, screening photos or results.

Model and limitations

The app uses a HAM10000-trained EfficientNet model with TensorFlow Lite. Skin-color filtering and segmentation are imperfect and can confuse objects with skin. No clinical accuracy is guaranteed. Read the medical disclaimer before interpreting predictions.

Privacy and support

Read Privacy & Data Consent for data collection, storage, sign-in connections and deletion choices. Contact the project team or institution that supplied your app for support. A dedicated public contact has not yet been supplied for this development version.
