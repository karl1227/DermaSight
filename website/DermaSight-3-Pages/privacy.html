Privacy & Data Consent

How DermaSight handles your information

Development version · Updated October 30, 2026

Describes the current prototype. Public contact and hosting details are pending finalization by the project team.

Scope and responsibility

This notice describes the current DermaSight educational prototype. The team providing your app operates its account service. Contact that team through your app distributor or institution for questions, access, correction, and deletion. A dedicated public contact and final hosting details have not yet been supplied; the team must finalize these before public distribution.

Information collected

Profiles contain name, email, sex, account identifiers and sign-in method. A screening may contain a name/sex snapshot, age, lesion location, occupation, history of sunburn, symptom answers, an image, predictions, confidence scores, and timestamps. Provide only information and images you are authorized to use. Avoid identifying surroundings and other people when unnecessary.

Purposes

Profile data populates forms and supports authentication. Screening data supports educational image analysis and local history. Occupation and sunburn answers provide context; they do not change the current image model prediction. Ownership identifiers separate guest sessions and registered accounts.

Local processing and storage

Image checks, segmentation and TensorFlow Lite inference run on your device. SQLite stores saved screening records; local files store images. This implementation does not upload scan photos/results, automatically train models with your images, or synchronize screening history across devices. Signing in elsewhere does not restore another device’s local scans.

Where your information is stored

  • On your phone: SQLite through op-sqlite stores screening details, guest sessions, predictions and history. Images are saved as local files.
  • Secure phone storage: login/session credentials use the platform secure credential store, including iOS Keychain.
  • On the account server: a separate SQLite database stores account profiles, linked identities, salted password hashes and authentication sessions. In the current development setup, this server runs on the project Mac.
  • Cloud storage: no cloud database, cloud image storage or automatic screening backup is configured.

Google Sign-In verifies identity; it does not store your screening images or history. Signing in on another phone does not restore this phone’s saved scans. Hosting these public information pages does not move the account server or upload screening data to that website.

Account service and Google

Registration sends account details and the password to the account service, which stores salted password hashes rather than plaintext passwords. Google Sign-In sends a Google identity token to that service for verification; it does not grant access to your Gmail inbox. The server stores profiles, linked identities and hashed session identifiers. Google applies its own privacy practices to its sign-in service.

Guests and account changes

Guest mode does not create a registered account. Guest scans belong to the current local guest session. Creating a new account from that session can transfer its scans to that account. Signing in to an existing account does not automatically adopt unrelated guest scans. Exiting guest mode ends access to that guest session; it does not guarantee immediate erasure of its stored data.

Permissions and connectivity

Camera permission supports capture. Gallery selection uses the platform photo picker and any required photo permission. Manage permissions in device settings. Internet is needed for account sign-in, profile updates and browser pages. Local inference does not require internet once the app and models are installed. Revoking permission does not erase existing records.

Internet connection checks

The Online/Offline indicator makes small connectivity requests to Google’s connectivity-check service while the app is active. That service receives ordinary connection information such as your IP address; no account identifiers, screening photos or results are sent with this check. A blocked check endpoint can make the indicator report Offline even when another service is reachable.

Retention and deletion

You may disable future history saving in Settings and delete individual reports or all saved records belonging to your current session owner. Disabling history or signing out does not delete existing records. Deletion attempts to remove image files; cleanup failures or device backups may leave copies. Account records remain on the server until the project team removes them; there is no in-app account deletion button. The local administrative tool creates backups before account deletion; the team must separately manage backup retention and deletion.

Security and development limits

Credentials use the platform credential store; history queries are owner-scoped. App-sandbox protection is not a claim of application-level database encryption or guaranteed security. Development builds may use HTTP on a trusted local network and should not carry real sensitive data over untrusted networks. Public account deployment requires HTTPS and operational safeguards. Shared/modified devices and backups can expose data.

Access, correction and rights

View history and edit supported report/profile fields in the app. Contact the team providing your app for server account access, correction, deletion, or backup questions. Applicable rights and exceptions depend on governing law and circumstances. Consult the Philippine National Privacy Commission for data-subject rights. This notice does not claim regulatory certification.

Children and other people

The age field is screening context, not parental consent or age verification. A parent or lawful guardian should oversee use involving children. Do not submit another person’s health information without appropriate authority and consent. The team must establish safeguards for its actual participants and deployment.

Website and external services

These static pages do not include scan/account data in their links, request photos, or contain application analytics/advertising scripts. Your browser and host still receive connection details such as IP address and request headers; hosting security features may log requests or set cookies. External links follow the destination’s privacy practices.

Consent and updates

Read this notice and the medical disclaimer before screening. Agree and Continue stores a local first-run acknowledgment, not a signed medical-consent record. You may stop using the app, revoke permissions, disable future history saving, and request deletion as described above. Opening a browser page does not record consent or delete data. The team must update disclosures when implementation or data practices change.
