Medical Disclaimer

Educational screening with clear limitations

Development version · Updated October 30, 2026

Describes the current prototype. Public contact and hosting details are pending finalization by the project team.

Purpose

DermaSight is an educational prototype for preliminary skin-lesion awareness. It is not a diagnosis, medical consultation, treatment recommendation or replacement for examination by a qualified healthcare professional. Do not use it to rule out cancer or conclude that a lesion is safe.

Supported classes

The HAM10000-trained EfficientNet TensorFlow Lite classifier predicts among Melanoma, Basal Cell Carcinoma, Actinic Keratoses, Melanocytic Nevi, Benign Keratosis, Dermatofibroma and Vascular Lesions. It does not recognize every condition or establish a clinical diagnosis.

Confidence and risk labels

Confidence is a model score among supported classes, not your probability of cancer or a guarantee. High Risk, Medium Risk and Low Risk are educational display categories, not validated clinical risk estimates. No result can establish or exclude disease. Reported model accuracy does not establish accuracy for a particular person, phone, photograph, or the complete app workflow.

Image validation limitations

The current skin filter uses color and coverage rules. Wood, leather, clothing and skin-colored objects can pass, while genuine skin can be rejected. The classifier can produce confident lesion predictions for unrelated objects. Quality checks and automatic lesion crops can fail or omit context. These checks do not establish clinical suitability.

Seeking care

Have a qualified healthcare professional assess new, growing, bleeding, changing, persistently itchy or otherwise concerning spots regardless of the result. Do not delay care, start/stop treatment or rely on this app to monitor a suspected cancer. Diagnosis may require examination and tests such as biopsy. For a medical emergency, seek local emergency care.

Variation and unsupported uses

Lighting, focus, compression, skin tone, camera hardware, lesion size, framing and differences from training data can affect results. The app provides no live clinician monitoring or emergency service. Occupation, sun exposure and symptom entries do not by themselves establish a diagnosis.

Processing and saved records

Image inference runs on the phone and saved screening records remain local. There is no automatic cloud backup or cross-device history synchronization. Google Sign-In is an account sign-in method and does not store screening images. See Privacy & Data Consent for the account-server and local-storage details.

Acknowledgment

Using the educational screening workflow means acknowledging these limitations. This does not waive privacy rights or claim regulatory approval, clinical validation or fitness for a medical purpose. The project team must obtain appropriate professional and institutional review before clinical deployment.
